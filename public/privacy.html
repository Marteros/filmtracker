Privacy Policy — Frame

Last updated: 2026-11-17

Frame is a personal movie journal web app. This policy explains what data Frame handles, how it is used, and what choices you have.

1. Data stored on your device

  • Your collection and settings (such as movies, watch status, ratings, notes, view preferences, and app settings) are stored locally in your browser (for example in localStorage and related browser storage) on your device.
  • Frame does not require you to create a Frame account and does not operate its own user database for your collection.
  • If you clear your browser/site data, uninstall the app data, or lose access to the device/browser profile, locally stored data may be lost unless you have created a backup/export.

2. TMDb (movie information)

  • To show movie information, posters, search results, and recommendations, Frame requests data from The Movie Database (TMDb) through a proxy service configured for the app.
  • Requests sent for this purpose may include technical information necessary to deliver the content (such as the requested movie/search query and standard network/request data like your IP address as seen by the proxy/TMDb). Frame does not intentionally send your local notes, ratings, or full collection to TMDb.
  • TMDb’s handling of data is subject to TMDb’s own terms and privacy practices.

3. Trakt (optional)

  • If you choose to connect Trakt in Settings, Frame uses Trakt’s OAuth authorization to access your Trakt account with your permission.
  • What Frame accesses: your Trakt watch history and watchlist (to import them into Frame). This is a one-way sync into Frame; Frame does not send your local notes/ratings/collection to Trakt.
  • Tokens and settings: Trakt authorization data/tokens and related settings are stored locally on your device to keep you signed in and perform syncs you request.
  • Disconnecting: you can disconnect Trakt in Settings, which removes stored Trakt credentials from the app on that device. You can also revoke Frame’s access from your Trakt account settings.
  • Trakt’s handling of data is subject to Trakt’s own terms and privacy policy.

Frame uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt.

4. Backups (import/export)

  • Frame lets you export your data to a file and import it back. Exported backup files are created and handled by you; you are responsible for where you store and share them.

5. What Frame does not do

  • Frame does not sell your personal data.
  • Frame does not intentionally collect your movie notes/ratings for advertising.
  • Frame does not upload your collection to a Frame-operated server as part of normal use (other than the third-party requests described above, such as TMDb proxy requests and optional Trakt API requests you initiate).

6. Third-party services and hosting

  • TMDb: movie data and images are provided via TMDb/proxy requests (see section 2).
  • Trakt: optional account connection and data import (see section 3).
  • Hosting: if you use the hosted version of Frame (for example on GitHub Pages), the hosting provider may process standard technical data (such as IP address, browser/user-agent, and request logs) to serve the site, according to their own policies.
  • Proxy: if a proxy is used to access TMDb, that proxy/provider may process request data necessary to forward requests and return responses.

7. Your choices and controls

  • Use without Trakt: Trakt is optional; you can use Frame without connecting it.
  • Sync on demand: Trakt data is fetched when you choose to sign in/sync.
  • Disconnect/revoke: disconnect in Settings and/or revoke access in your Trakt account.
  • Export/delete local data: export a backup if you want a copy, and clear site/app/browser data to remove locally stored data from your device.

8. Children’s privacy

Frame is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). Do not use the app in violation of applicable age requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date will reflect the latest version.
